03 / Operational notes

The starter sends short audio recordings to the backend. The backend transcribes speech, buffers incomplete phrases, and translates completed sentences. It does not perform automatic profanity, nudity, violence, or religious-content classification. Human review remains required. This starter does not store transcripts in a database.
